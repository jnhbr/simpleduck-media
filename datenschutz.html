Privacy Policy – Simple Duck Uploader

Simple Duck Uploader is a private tool used only by the operator of the YouTube channels @simpleduck_de and @simpleduck_en to upload and schedule the channels' own educational videos via the YouTube Data API.

  • The tool accesses only these two channels, owned by the operator. It is not offered to other users.
  • It uploads video files, titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlist entries created by the operator. It does not read, store or share data of viewers or other YouTube users.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the operator's computer only and are never shared with third parties.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • The tool uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Datenschutz (Deutsch)

Simple Duck Uploader ist ein privates Werkzeug des Betreibers der YouTube-Kanäle @simpleduck_de und @simpleduck_en. Es lädt ausschliesslich die eigenen Lernvideos dieser Kanäle über die YouTube Data API hoch und plant sie. Es werden keine Daten von Zuschauerinnen und Zuschauern oder anderen Personen gelesen, gespeichert oder weitergegeben. Zugangsschlüssel bleiben lokal auf dem Computer des Betreibers.
